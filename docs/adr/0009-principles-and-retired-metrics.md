# Published principles, including retired metrics

The product publishes its principles and is held to them: no creator data in case studies or marketing; no viral-potential predictions or channel grades; outlier comparisons only against channels of a similar size; no growth promises in upgrade prompts; no AI processors receiving channel data; and only metrics that measure how a channel's own viewers respond.

Four metrics are retired: tags, SEO scores, keyword competition scores, and best-time-to-post advice. The incumbents still sell these. The reason they have in common is the one in the **Retired metric** entry in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md), and /principles also gives each one a reason of its own. Offering them, or feeding small creators comparisons with channels they will never resemble, is exactly the behaviour the product exists to refuse, so the refusal is recorded as a decision rather than left as an omission someone later "fixes."
