# No attention tricks, and the creator's own past as the benchmark

The product offers no attention tricks: no streaks, no red and green deltas, no growth alerts, and no confetti or celebration states. Each of them rewards checking the numbers rather than informing a decision about them, which is the habit the incumbents build and the product exists to refuse.

The creator's own past is the default frame on every screen. Comparisons with other channels appear only on the creator's request, in a research view, and never as a grade, a target or a rank. Outlier scores stay, because applied correctly they are a growth tool: they compare only against channels of a similar size (ADR 0009), and they wait for acceptance after the compliance audit (ADR 0004).

The reason: creators get the information they need to decide how they want to grow, not how YouTube's benchmarks persuade them to. This joins the published principles of ADR 0009, and it binds the web app (#2) and the Studio extension (#4) as well as the site, so a later change that adds a streak or a benchmark grade is visibly a breach of a decision rather than a feature.
