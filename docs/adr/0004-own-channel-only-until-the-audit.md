# Own-channel analytics only until the derived-metrics audit

Version 1 shows a creator only their own channel's authorized data. Outlier scores, revenue estimates for other channels, and competitor history beyond 30 days are derived metrics or long-held non-authorized data, which YouTube allows only for API clients accepted under its 2026 derived-metrics policy after a compliance audit. The audit needs a live product to review and has no published timeline, so it is applied for after the Studio extension ships, and the outlier engine waits for acceptance.
