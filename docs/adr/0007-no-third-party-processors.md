# No third-party scripts, no AI processors, first-party analytics only

The product loads no third-party scripts or ad pixels, sends no channel data to any AI processor, never sells or shares data, and never uses a creator's data in case studies or marketing. Product analytics run on a self-hosted OpenPanel with first-party events only, disclosed in the privacy policy.

vidIQ and ViewStats both state their ad tracking may count as a "sale" under US state privacy law, and Morningfame, the closest privacy-positioned competitor, lists OpenAI and Google Cloud Vision as processors that may receive channel data. "No third-party scripts and no AI processors" is a claim none of them can make.
