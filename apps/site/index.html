Honest numbers, and nothing else.

  • Engaged views, kept apart

    In August 2026 YouTube changed how it counts a view. Stillstats also shows engaged views, counted the stricter way YouTube used before, so your numbers before and after the change count views the same way.

  • No tags, no SEO scores

    Tags, SEO scores, keyword competition scores and best-time-to-post advice each measure something other than how your own viewers respond to your videos. Stillstats leaves them out, and the principles say why.

  • No growth promises

    No channel grades, no viral predictions, and no upgrade prompt that promises you growth. Stillstats tells you what happened on your channel, plainly.

See your engaged views inside YouTube Studio.

The free Stillstats extension for Chrome and Firefox shows your engaged views in three places in YouTube Studio: the latest video card, the Content list, and each video's analytics page. If you turn on YouTube access in the extension's popup, it also shows them beside YouTube's view counts on your own videos on public YouTube pages.

It reads every number from Stillstats, never from the Studio page. It runs on YouTube Studio, and on public YouTube pages only when you turn on YouTube access.

Privacy is the proof.

  • No scripts but YouTube's player

    The site and the app load no ad pixel and no script from another company. The one exception is YouTube's own video player, on a video's page in the app. Product analytics run on a server Stillstats operates itself, with first-party events only.

  • No AI processors

    Your channel data never goes to an AI service. Not for summaries, not for suggestions, and not for training.

  • Published retention windows

    Each kind of data has a time limit, and the limits are public.

What other tools publish about your data.

Each claim below comes from the company's own published page. Each one links that page and shows the date it was checked, so you can read the source yourself.

Social Blade

Social Blade's terms say it does not delete the statistics it shows. A creator stops collection only by making the profile private.

Terms of Service, section 9. Checked October 16, 2026.

vidIQ

vidIQ's privacy policy says it discloses identifiers and network activity to advertising networks and analytics providers. It says these disclosures may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" under applicable laws.

Privacy Policy, section 2.3. Checked October 16, 2026.

ViewStats

ViewStats's privacy policy says it has sold or shared identifiers, internet activity and IP location with its analytics and advertising partners in the past 12 months, as applicable law defines those terms.

Privacy Policy, U.S. Supplemental Privacy Policy. Checked October 16, 2026.

Morningfame

Morningfame's privacy policy says it shares the channel and video data it reads from YouTube with the providers it lists under hosting and backend infrastructure. That list includes OpenAI API and Google Cloud Vision AI.

Privacy Policy, YouTube API Services and Hosting and backend infrastructure. Checked October 16, 2026.
