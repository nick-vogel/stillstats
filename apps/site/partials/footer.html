Questions

What data does Stillstats read?

To sign you in, your Google name, email address and profile picture. For each channel you connect, YouTube's read-only channel data and analytics. Revenue only for a channel you give revenue consent to, in a separate step.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, Disconnect removes one channel and Delete me removes you and every channel you connected. Either way, the data is deleted within 24 hours, and the backups that hold it expire within 5 days. You can also remove Stillstats's access in your Google Account settings.

What does Stillstats Pro add?

It costs $7 a month or $70 a year for each channel*. Each new upload shows beside the average of your newest uploads of its format at the same age, over their first 7 and first 28 days, for each traffic source, such as browse features, search and suggested videos. Your impressions and click-through rate show beside your engaged views in your summary, charts, video list and each video's traffic sources. Your video list sorts by click-through rate or impressions, for all traffic sources or just one, and hides videos under a minimum number of impressions you set. The audience retention curves of up to 5 of your videos show on one chart. Stillstats reads each curve daily for the video's first 28 days, then monthly. The impressions and click-through rate history starts with up to 60 days before you subscribe, and grows each day. Stillstats keeps it while your subscription is active, and for 30 days after it ends.

* This is changing soon: $7 a month or $70 a year will cover up to 4 channels for one person.

Can I send a sponsor a video's numbers?

Yes. On a video's page, Sponsor report opens a page made to print with the video's figures over its first 7, 14 and 30 days, and the day its data runs through. You choose the figures, and Stillstats remembers your choice for the channel. It never includes revenue. It is free, and with Pro it can also include impressions and click-through rate. Save as PDF opens your browser's print dialog, and you send the file to your sponsor yourself.

There is no link to share with a sponsor. YouTube's developer policies limit who Stillstats may show your YouTube data to, and they do not say whether a sponsor can be one of them.

Stillstats's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stillstats's use of information received from the Stillstats extension will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
