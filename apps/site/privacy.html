Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Stillstats is a read-only analytics product for YouTube creators. MIDLO WEB DESIGN, LLC, a Virginia limited liability company, operates Stillstats. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean MIDLO WEB DESIGN, LLC. This policy explains what information Stillstats accesses, how it uses, stores and shares that information, and how you delete it. You agree to this policy when you sign in to Stillstats.

YouTube API Services and Google

Stillstats uses YouTube API Services to read your channel's data, with your permission. Google's own use of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Stillstats's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stillstats's use of information received from the Stillstats extension will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Stillstats accesses

Stillstats asks Google for your permission in up to three separate steps.

  • Sign in with Google. Stillstats receives your Google account ID, your name, your email address and your profile picture. It uses them to create your account and to show you who is signed in.
  • Connect a channel. Stillstats asks to view your YouTube account and to view your YouTube Analytics reports. It reads your channel's ID each time you connect the channel or give revenue consent, to know which channel the permission is for. It reads your channel's name, handle and creation date, your uploads list with each video's title, thumbnail, publish date, privacy status and length, and the analytics of your channel and your videos: views, engaged views, watch time, average percentage viewed, audience retention, traffic sources, the split between subscribers and non-subscribers, and subscribers gained, for each day and in total over the window you pick, and on each video's page, for each day since it was published. For your channel as a whole, never for a single video, it also reads the countries, US states and cities your viewers watch from, the age group and gender of signed-in viewers, and the device types and operating systems they watch on, in total over the window you pick. It reads each video's format (video, Short or live stream) from the video's views since it was published, whatever window you pick. It also reads your channel's numbers for each day of the last 90 days, to know which days Google has finished counting. It also asks for your email address and basic account identity, so that Google can send it security alerts about your account.
  • Revenue consent, only if you choose to give it. Stillstats asks to view the monetary reports of YouTube Analytics. It reads the estimated revenue of your channel and of each video, with its sources, its revenue by day and your channel's revenue by format, over the window you pick and, on each video's page, since the video was published, and shows it in your channel summary, your video list and each video's page. If you never give revenue consent, Stillstats never reads revenue data.
  • A daily reach report. With the permission to view your YouTube Analytics reports, Stillstats asks YouTube to prepare a daily reach report for each channel you connect: the impressions and click-through rate of your videos. It asks when you connect the channel, and asks again each day until YouTube has it.
  • A Stillstats Pro channel's history. For a channel on Stillstats Pro, Stillstats downloads the channel's new reach reports each day, and on its first day every report YouTube still holds, up to 60 days. With the permission to view your YouTube account, it also asks YouTube once a day which of the videos in the history still exist.

Stillstats is read-only. It never changes anything on your YouTube channel, and it never asks for your YouTube password.

The Studio extension

The Stillstats extension for Chrome and Firefox works on studio.youtube.com and stillstats.com, and on www.youtube.com only if you turn that on.

On studio.youtube.com it reads the channel id and video ids on the page you have open, and the analytics period you select. It does not read the numbers, text or anything else on the page.

It sends stillstats.com the video ids, the period and Stillstats' own id for the channel you connected, with your Stillstats sign-in. It matches that channel to the page on your device, so the YouTube channel id stays in your browser. For the comparison on the latest video card, it also sends the video's format and how many recent uploads to compare. stillstats.com answers with your numbers from YouTube's official API. The rest of this policy covers how Stillstats handles them.

It sends a few usage events to stillstats.com: that it was installed, which kind of page showed a number or could not (a Studio page, or a channel page, watch page, feed or Shorts on www.youtube.com), how long it took your data to show and whether it came from a recent copy, when you turn on access to www.youtube.com and when you or your browser remove it, and when you click one of its links to stillstats.com. They carry no ids, names or email, and they go to Stillstats' own analytics, run on its own server. In Firefox, it sends them only if you allow it, when you install the extension or later in about:addons.

It never uses your YouTube sign-in, never reads YouTube's private endpoints, and never sells or shares anything it reads.

If you turn on "Show engaged views on youtube.com" in the extension's popup, the extension also works on www.youtube.com. There it reads the links to videos and channels on the page, and the page address. It asks Stillstats for the videos of the channels you connected, with your Stillstats sign-in, and matches them on your device, so no video id from www.youtube.com leaves your browser. It reads no number from the page, and does not use your YouTube sign-in. You can turn it off in the popup or in your browser's extension settings.

What Stillstats stores

  • Your account: your Google account ID, name, email address and the address of your profile picture.
  • Your sign-in: the tokens Google issued when you signed in and the permissions you granted. These are an access token, an ID token and, when Google issues one, a refresh token. The access token and the refresh token are encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305 before they are stored. Stillstats keeps them until you delete your account.
  • Your sessions: the browser user agent of each signed-in session. Stillstats does not store your IP address with a session.
  • Your display preferences: page size, which columns and charts show, whether the charts show daily numbers or a running total, your currency, whether the video list shows unlisted and private videos, whether the sidebar is open, and your theme (Light, Dark or System). They stay until you reset them in Settings or delete yourself from the product.
  • Each connected channel: the channel's ID, the permissions you granted and when you granted them, and the access and refresh tokens Google issued for the channel. These tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are stored.
  • Keyed one-way hashes of your Google account ID and of two identifiers of your refresh token. When Google sends a security alert, Stillstats finds your connection by these hashes.
  • Each feedback message you send from Stillstats: its type, its text, the name of the screen you sent it from, the app version, and a reply address only when you choose to give one. It holds no channel ID and no video ID.
  • For each connected channel, the ID of the request for its daily reach report and when YouTube created it. The free tier stores nothing from the report itself.
  • For a channel on Stillstats Pro, its reach history: the impressions and click-through rate of each video, for each day as YouTube counts it and for each traffic source type, such as browse features, YouTube search or suggested videos, and which days the history holds. It stores nothing finer, such as the search terms or the videos that suggested yours.
  • For a channel on Stillstats Pro, the audience retention curve of each of its videos: how many of the video's viewers are still watching at each point in the video, and when Stillstats read the curve. Stillstats keeps the curves for as long as it keeps the channel's reach history.
  • For each connected channel, the figures you chose to include in its sponsor report. It holds no numbers.
  • For a channel that has subscribed to Stillstats Pro, its subscription: the Stripe customer ID and subscription ID, the plan, the subscription's status, the end of the paid period and, once the subscription ends, the purge date of its history. It also holds the Stripe invoice ID of each payment, so that each payment counts once in the product analytics described below. It holds no email, name, card or amount.
  • The analytics results Stillstats shows you, and your channel's name and handle, for a short time only. See "How long Stillstats keeps your data" below.

How Stillstats uses your data

Stillstats uses your Google user data only to provide the features you see in Stillstats: showing which channel is connected and its name, in the channel picker and in Settings, and its handle in the channel picker; using your channel's creation date as the earliest day you can pick; showing your channel's numbers, and your videos' titles and numbers, in your channel summary; showing each video's title, thumbnail, length and numbers in your video list and on each video's page, and its publish date, privacy status and format in your video list, where you can also filter by format; using each video's publish date to count its numbers from the day it was published; showing a video's numbers over its first 7, 14 and 30 days in its sponsor report; and showing your channel's and each video's estimated revenue if you gave revenue consent, in your channel summary, your video list and each video's page; and, for a channel on Stillstats Pro, storing its impressions and click-through rate as history, showing them in your channel summary and its charts, in your video list, where you can also sort and filter by them, in each video's traffic sources and in its sponsor report, showing each new upload beside the average of your newest uploads of its format over the same days since publish, storing the audience retention curve of each of its videos, to show up to 5 of them on one chart, and checking which videos in the history still exist, to delete the history and the retention curve of a deleted one. Stillstats does not use your data for advertising, to determine creditworthiness or for lending, or to train any AI model. We never use your channel or its data in case studies or marketing.

No person reads your data unless you ask us to, for example in a support request, or unless it is necessary for security, such as investigating a bug or abuse, or to comply with the law.

How Stillstats shares your data

We do not sell your data. We share it with no one beyond the infrastructure that delivers Stillstats, described below, and Stripe, only when you open the tip link or one of Stillstats Pro's Stripe pages. Stillstats sends no data to an AI processor, an advertising platform or a data broker. Stillstats loads no ad pixels, and no third-party script except YouTube's video player, which loads only when you ask for it, as described below.

We transfer your data only in these cases:

  • to comply with the law;
  • if Stillstats is sold or merged, and only after we ask for and receive your explicit consent first.

Stillstats sends requests to Google's APIs on your behalf, and Google receives those requests. Requests to stillstats.com, and the responses to them, pass through Cloudflare, which delivers the site and protects it from attacks.

Video thumbnails in the app load from YouTube's image servers on ytimg.com, such as i.ytimg.com. Your browser fetches them straight from YouTube, the same way YouTube Studio does. A video's page in the app also loads YouTube's video player when the video has audience retention to show, from youtube.com and youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. While that page is open, YouTube's player runs in it, and Google receives what any embedded YouTube player sends it, such as your IP address and the video on the page. The player can set cookies and can show YouTube's ads. Google's Privacy Policy applies to it, and YouTube's help page on privacy-enhanced mode says what that mode changes. No other page in the app runs a script from YouTube or anyone else.

Tips go through Stripe. The tip link opens Stripe's own page. When you open the tip link, Stripe receives a Stillstats account ID, an opaque code that holds no name or email address, so that Stillstats knows which account tipped. Stripe receives that account ID even if you do not pay. If you tip, Stripe also receives what you type on Stripe's page. Stillstats loads no Stripe script, and it stores only the fact of the tip. When you delete your account, Stripe keeps its own record of the payment under its own obligations.

Stillstats Pro goes through Stripe too. Subscribing opens Stripe Checkout, and managing or cancelling a subscription opens the Stripe Customer Portal, each Stripe's own page. When you open Checkout, Stripe receives the channel's Stillstats ID, an opaque code that holds no name, email address or YouTube channel ID, so that Stillstats knows which channel the subscription is for, the plan you chose, and, for a channel that subscribed before, its earlier Stripe customer. Stripe sells Stillstats Pro as the merchant of record, through Stripe Managed Payments, so Stripe also receives what you type on its pages, and it keeps its own records of your payments under its own obligations. Stillstats loads no Stripe script, and it stores only what "What Stillstats stores" lists.

Product analytics and error reports

Stillstats counts how people use the site and the product with OpenPanel, which we host on our own servers. No OpenPanel data goes to a third party. The site records page views, clicks on the sign-in button, on Stillstats Pro's "Subscribe" button and on the links in the site's navigation, and a few actions in the thumbnail preview tool, such as loading an image or turning on grayscale. No tool event carries an image, a file name or a title you type. The product records a small set of actions, such as clicking to connect a channel, to disconnect a channel or to delete your account. It also records how long each main screen takes to show and to answer what you ask, and whether the answer came from a recent copy. No event carries a channel ID, an account ID or an email address. If you click "Support Stillstats" and leave a tip, that tip event carries the amount and currency, and no account ID. Stillstats sends it when Stripe confirms the tip, so it carries no IP address or user agent. If your channel is on Stillstats Pro, each payment of its subscription sends a payment event, which carries the amount before tax and the currency, and no account ID or channel ID. Stillstats sends it when Stripe confirms the payment, so it carries no IP address or user agent either. Every other event carries your browser's IP address and user agent, like any web request, which OpenPanel uses to tell visits apart and to estimate your country.

When something breaks, Stillstats sends an error report to GlitchTip, which we also host on our own servers. Before a report leaves the process, Stillstats removes tokens, channel IDs, analytics values, request headers, cookies and request bodies from it. Stillstats also sends GlitchTip a timing record of some requests to its server: the kind of request, its status and how long it took. A timing record carries no channel ID, video ID, account ID, email, token or analytics value.

Stillstats also counts how many people use the product each day, each week and each month. When you read your channel's numbers, in the app or through the extension, Stillstats records the date on which you did, and nothing else. Only the counts go to OpenPanel, with no account ID, IP address or user agent. This record does not depend on the extension's usage events setting.

Cookies and device storage

Stillstats places two cookies in your browser. Both are strictly necessary.

  • __Secure-better-auth.session_token keeps you signed in. It expires after 7 days without use, or when you sign out. Scripts on the page cannot read it.
  • __Secure-better-auth.state protects the sign-in from forgery. It exists only while you sign in, and it expires after 5 minutes.

Stillstats also keeps a copy of your theme in your browser's local storage for stillstats.com, under the name theme, so that the app's pages show your theme as they load. The copy holds only light or dark, and only while your theme is Light or Dark. When your theme is System, for example after you choose System or reset your preferences in Settings, Stillstats removes the copy. It stays when you sign out or delete your account. To remove it then, clear your browser's data for stillstats.com. It is not a cookie, so your browser does not send it with any request.

OpenPanel sets no cookie and keeps no identifier on your device. Stillstats sets no advertising cookies and no third-party cookies. The one exception is YouTube's video player on a video's page: there, YouTube can store and read information on your device under Google's Privacy Policy. On every other page, no third party stores or reads information on your device.

How long Stillstats keeps your data

  • Analytics results are cached for 24 hours or less. On the free tier, Stillstats never keeps them as history. On Stillstats Pro, it also keeps a channel's impressions and click-through rate as history, for as long as the next item says.
  • A Stillstats Pro channel's reach history and its audience retention curves are kept while its subscription is active. The subscription ends at the end of its paid period, not when you press Cancel. Both then stay readable for 30 days, until the purge date, and Stillstats downloads no new days and reads no new curves. On the purge date, Stillstats deletes them. If you subscribe again before then, Stillstats keeps both and fills in the history's missing days from the reports YouTube still holds. The record of the subscription and of its payments stays while the channel is connected.
  • When a video is deleted on YouTube, Stillstats deletes its reach history and its audience retention curve at the next daily check. A private or unlisted video keeps both.
  • Your account, your sessions and your sign-in tokens stay until you delete your account. When you delete it, Stillstats deletes them at once.
  • The record of the days on which you used Stillstats is kept for 30 days, then deleted. When you delete your account, Stillstats deletes it at once.
  • A feedback message stays until you delete your account, or until one year after it is handled, whichever comes first. When you delete your account, Stillstats deletes your messages at once.
  • A connected channel stays until you disconnect it, delete your account or revoke Stillstats's access at Google.
  • When you disconnect a channel, delete your account or revoke access at Google, Stillstats deletes the channel's live data within 24 hours. The backups that hold it expire within 5 days.
  • When you disconnect a paid channel, revoke access at Google or delete your account, its reach history, its audience retention curves and the record of its subscription and of its payments go with the channel's other data, on the windows above. Stillstats also cancels the channel's subscription with Stripe at once, with a refund of the unused share of its latest payment, as the Terms of Service set out, and keeps the Stripe subscription ID only until Stripe has accepted the cancellation and the refund.
  • Revoking access at Google deletes the channel's data, not your account. To delete your account, delete it inside Stillstats.
  • To prove that a deletion happened, Stillstats keeps a record of it for 24 months. The record holds a keyed one-way hash of the channel ID, the number of rows deleted, the reason and the times. It holds no channel ID and no analytics data.

Deleting your data and revoking access

You can delete your data inside Stillstats at any time.

  • Disconnect a channel. Stillstats revokes its access at Google and deletes the channel's data. For a paid channel, it also cancels the subscription at once and refunds the unused share of its latest payment, as the Terms of Service set out.
  • Delete your account. Stillstats disconnects every channel and deletes your account, your sessions and your sign-in tokens. For each paid channel it deletes, it also cancels the subscription at once and refunds the unused share of its latest payment, as the Terms of Service set out.

Deleting data stored by Stillstats does not affect data stored by YouTube. To delete data on YouTube, use YouTube or another authorized app that can delete that data.

In addition to deleting data inside Stillstats, you can revoke Stillstats's access to your data on the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Stillstats checks every connection each day, and Google also alerts Stillstats to some revocations. When Stillstats finds a revocation, it deletes the channel's data on the windows above. Your account stays until you delete it.

You can also email privacy@stillstats.com to ask us to delete your data. We delete it within 7 days of your request.

Security

Stillstats serves every page over HTTPS. It encrypts the access and refresh tokens Google issues before it stores them, and its database keeps each account's data apart from every other account's data.
