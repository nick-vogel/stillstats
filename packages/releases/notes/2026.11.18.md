## What changed

- Stillstats has a free [thumbnail preview tool](https://stillstats.com/tools/thumbnail-preview). Load up to three thumbnails and see each one at the sizes YouTube actually shows it, in grayscale, and as a Shorts thumbnail. You don't need to sign in, and your images stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
- "Thumbnail preview" in the sidebar opens the tool in a new tab.
- The menu at the bottom of the sidebar now lists Mid-Roll Manager as its free tool.
- Every page on stillstats.com has a navigation bar, with the thumbnail preview tool, Mid-Roll Manager and the Studio extension for Chrome and Firefox.
- The thumbnail preview tool uses the full width of your window, and a link above its title takes you home, or back to your account when you're signed in.
- On a phone, the app's screens, "Send feedback" and "Support Stillstats" sit behind one Menu button in the header.
