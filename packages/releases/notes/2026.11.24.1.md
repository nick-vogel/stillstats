## What changed

- Stillstats now measures how fast your data loads and displays to find opportunities to improve the application. These measurements carry no channel, video or account ID.
- The [Privacy Policy](https://stillstats.com/privacy) now describes these measurements.
- The homepage says the extension also runs on public YouTube pages, but only when you turn on YouTube access.
- The note on coming pricing, on the homepage, in the FAQ and on the upgrade screen, now says "for one person".
