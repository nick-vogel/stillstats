## What changed

- When you are signed in, the extension's popup shows a "Send feedback" link under "Open Stillstats".
- It opens the feedback form in Stillstats in a new tab, where you can say what is wrong or share an idea.
- Messages sent this way are marked as coming from the extension.
- The extension now measures how long your data takes to show, and whether it came from a recent copy, to find opportunities to improve Stillstats. These measurements carry no channel, video or account ID.
- In Firefox, the extension sends these measurements only if you allow it, when you install the extension or later in about:addons.
