## What changed

- The [Privacy Policy](https://stillstats.com/privacy) now says what the Studio extension reads on www.youtube.com once you turn that on in its popup, and that no video id from those pages leaves your browser.
- The Privacy Policy also names the usage events the extension sends about www.youtube.com: which kind of page showed a number or could not, and when you turn access to www.youtube.com on or off.
- On the Releases page, you can click anywhere on a release to open it.
