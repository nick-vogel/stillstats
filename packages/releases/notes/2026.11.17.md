## What changed

- Your channel summary shows a marker under the chart for each video you published in the window. Hover over a marker, or move to it with the keyboard, to see the video's title and publish day.
- When uploads fall on the same day, or too close to tell apart, their markers join into one tile. Hover over the tile, or click or tap it, to see each video with its own date.
- Each chart draws a faint dashed line on every upload day, so you can see what your numbers did after each video. When more than one chart shows, a second row of markers sits under the first chart.
- The markers follow the "Show unlisted and private" switch. A scheduled video that is not out yet gets no marker.
- "Send feedback" in the sidebar opens a short form. Choose whether something is wrong, you have an idea, or something else, write your message, and add a reply address if you want an answer. You can send up to 10 messages a day.
- The [Privacy Policy](https://stillstats.com/privacy) now says what a feedback message holds and how long Stillstats keeps it.
