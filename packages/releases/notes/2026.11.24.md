## What changed

- Each video's page has a "Sponsor report": the video's numbers over its first 7, 14 and 30 days, on one page you save as a PDF and send. You pick the figures it shows, and it never shows revenue. On Stillstats Pro, it can also show impressions and click-through rate.
- On Stillstats Pro, the new Retention page shows the audience retention curves of up to 5 of your videos on one chart. Stillstats reads each curve daily for a video's first 28 days, then monthly.
- On Stillstats Pro, your video list sorts by click-through rate or impressions, shows them for one traffic source, and hides videos under a minimum number of impressions you set.
- On Stillstats Pro, each video's page shows its impressions and click-through rate as cards at the top, and its traffic sources table lists the most impressions first.
- When you subscribe to Stillstats Pro, your impressions and click-through rate history starts to download at once, not the next day.
- Some channels' impressions and click-through rate history stopped at an early day. It now fills in every day YouTube still holds.
- After the Retention or Audience page, the "Videos" link opens your video list with the date range you had.
- The [Privacy Policy](https://stillstats.com/privacy) now describes the stored retention curves and the figures you choose for a sponsor report.
- Nothing you use went away. Stillstats now records a subscription in a way the previous version cannot read, so this release cannot be rolled back.
