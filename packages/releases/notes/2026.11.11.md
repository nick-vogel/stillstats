## What changed

- Every Stillstats release now has its own page under Releases, so you can read what changed and when.
- The sidebar has a "What's new" link. It opens a screen that shows the new features as they ship.
- The account menu shows the version of Stillstats you are using, and links to that version's release page.
- The [Privacy Policy](https://stillstats.com/privacy) now says more clearly that the audience breakdowns represent the channel, not each video.
