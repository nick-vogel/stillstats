## What changed

- A video's page plays the video beside its audience retention charts. Click, tap or press Enter on either chart, and the player moves to the start of that moment. The video starts only when you press the player's own play button.
- While the video plays, a line on both retention charts shows where it is.
- A Short plays in a tall 9:16 frame.
- If a video does not allow embedding, you will see a link pointing to watch it on YouTube.
- Each retention chart's explanation now sits under its chart.
- The [Privacy Policy](https://stillstats.com/privacy) now says what YouTube's player receives on a video's page. It also says that Stillstats keeps a record of the days you use it for 30 days, to count its daily, weekly and monthly users, and that the tip event and those counts carry no IP address or user agent.
