## What changed

- You can choose Light, Dark or System under Theme in Settings, or from the menu at the bottom of the sidebar. Stillstats saves your choice and shows your theme from the moment a page loads. System follows your device, as before.
- The Profile screen is now Settings. The row at the bottom of the sidebar says "Settings" above your email, and opens a menu with Settings, the free tools and Sign out. An old link to Profile still opens Settings.
- A custom date range now opens a calendar under the date menu: one month on a phone, two on a wider screen. You can pick only days that Stillstats has numbers for. After you apply a range, a small button in the bar shows it, and you can press it to change the range.
- The Audience screen fits its cards into columns by height, so far less of the screen is empty. A card's place can change with the channel and the date range.
- The [Privacy Policy](https://stillstats.com/privacy) now says that your theme is saved with your other display preferences, and that your browser keeps a copy of it so that pages load in your theme.
