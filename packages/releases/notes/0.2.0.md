## What changed

- Turn on "Show engaged views on youtube.com" in the extension's popup, and your browser asks for access to www.youtube.com. It stays off until you turn it on.
- Your videos then show their engaged views next to YouTube's view count: on your channel's Home, Videos, Shorts and Live tabs, on the watch page and in its sidebar, in your home feed and search results, and under your channel name in the Shorts player.
- Hover the number to see the full figure and the day it runs through.
- The extension matches the videos on the page against your own videos in your browser, so no video id from www.youtube.com leaves it. Videos from other channels show no number.
- You can turn it off in the popup or in your browser's extension settings.
