## What changed

- Stillstats Pro is here, for $7 a month or $70 a year for each channel\*. There is no trial. The channel's owner subscribes in Settings, or from the upgrade screen.
- On Stillstats Pro, each new upload shows beside the average of your newest uploads of its format at the same age, over their first 7 and first 28 days, for each traffic source.
- On Stillstats Pro, your channel summary, its charts, your video list and each video's traffic sources show your impressions and click-through rate beside your engaged views.
- The history starts with up to 60 days before you subscribe, and grows each day.
- The upgrade screen shows what Pro adds, what it costs, and how many of your uploads are ready for review today.
- An "Upgrade" item in the sidebar opens the upgrade screen for the channel on view. You can hide it, and it stays hidden.
- The Pro card on the homepage has a "Subscribe" button.
- "Manage subscription" in Settings opens Stripe's page, where you change your card, switch between monthly and yearly, or cancel. A cancelled subscription stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
- When a subscription ends, its history stays readable for 30 days, then is deleted. Subscribe again before then and Stillstats keeps it and fills in the missing days.
- Disconnecting a paid channel, removing Stillstats's access at Google or deleting your account cancels the subscription at once and refunds the unused share of your latest payment. Credit from a switch between monthly and yearly is not refunded.
- The [Terms](https://stillstats.com/terms) and the [Privacy Policy](https://stillstats.com/privacy) now describe Stillstats Pro: its price, what it stores, how long it keeps it, and Stripe's part as the seller.

\* This is changing soon: $7 a month or $70 a year will cover up to 4 channels for an individual account.
