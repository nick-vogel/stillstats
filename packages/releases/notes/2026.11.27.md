## What changed

- When you send feedback, Stillstats sends itself a notice email through Cloudflare Email Service. The [Privacy Policy](https://stillstats.com/privacy) now names it and says what that email holds: the type of message, when you sent it and a link to it. It never holds your message's text, your reply address, your account or your channel.
- The homepage, the Privacy Policy and the screen where you connect your channel now say that Stillstats never sells your data and shares it only with the services the Privacy Policy names.
- The homepage now says where the extension shows your engaged views on public YouTube pages when you turn on YouTube access.
- The Privacy Policy and the Terms no longer load Cloudflare's script that hides email addresses from spam robots.
