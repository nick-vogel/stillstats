---
title: New Stillstats Pro features
---

- Compare the audience retention curves of up to 5 of your videos on one chart, on the new Retention page.
- Sort your video list by click-through rate or impressions, for all traffic sources or just one, and hide videos under a minimum number of impressions.
- See each video's impressions and click-through rate as cards at the top of its page.
- Add impressions and click-through rate to a video's sponsor report.
