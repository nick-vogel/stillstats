title: Stillstats Pro
screen: /app/upgrade

Stillstats Pro shows your impressions and click-through rate beside your engaged views, and puts each new upload beside the average of your newest uploads at the same age. It keeps your channel's history while you subscribe. Pro costs $7 a month or $70 a year for each channel\*. Open "Upgrade" in the sidebar to see what it adds.

\* This is changing soon: $7 a month or $70 a year will cover up to 4 channels for one person.
