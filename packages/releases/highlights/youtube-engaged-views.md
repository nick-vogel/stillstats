---
title: Your engaged views on YouTube itself
---

Turn on "Show engaged views on youtube.com" in the Stillstats extension's popup, and your videos show their engaged views next to YouTube's view count: on your channel, the watch page, your home feed, search and Shorts. The extension matches your videos in your browser, so no video id from www.youtube.com leaves it. [Read what changed in the extension](https://stillstats.com/releases/0.2.0).
