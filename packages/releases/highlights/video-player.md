---
title: Watch the moment your viewers left
---

A video's page now plays the video beside its audience retention charts. Click, tap or press Enter on either chart, and the player moves to the start of that moment, so you can watch what played as viewers left. A line on both charts follows the playback. The video player is YouTube's privacy-enhanced video viewer. Click anywhere on the chart, then press 'play!' For more information about YouTube's player and the information it collects, check out the [Privacy Policy](https://stillstats.com/privacy).
